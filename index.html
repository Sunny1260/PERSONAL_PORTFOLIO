About

Hey, I'm Sunny. I graduated from Chaudhary Devi Lal University, Sirsa this June with a B.Tech in Computer Science, AI/ML specialization. Somewhere in the middle of that degree I got hooked on the moment a messy dataset turns into a chart someone can actually make a call from.

Across three internships I went from cleaning logistics data in Python to writing DAX measures for Power BI dashboards that real stakeholders were opening every week. I like the whole loop — pull the data, question it, model it, and present it in a way a non-technical team lead can actually trust.

Outside internship hours, I build small end-to-end projects: a spam classifier, a plant-disease detector, whatever dataset I get curious about on a given weekend. Mostly to keep the habit of shipping something, not just studying it.
